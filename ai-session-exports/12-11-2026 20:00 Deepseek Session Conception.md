If you invert it — build a beautiful UI first, or all five agents before any integration — you will run out of time with nothing working end-to-end. The single-company vertical slice in Phase 3 is your insurance policy.

Start right now with Phase 0, steps 1–3. The live skeleton and the scope note are the two things that make everything after them easier.
