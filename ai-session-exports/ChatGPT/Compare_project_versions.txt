3. Important issue: Company discovery is still not sufficiently restrictive
Needs attention
Remember our Ecom CFO problem?
The office version introduces an improved discovery endpoint, but I found something important.
The discovery route currently uses these queries:

US direct-to-consumer ecommerce brands.
American online retail consumer brands.
Ecommerce brands with founder/team information.

It also blocks known directories and platforms.
However, it does not implement a sufficiently strict business-type qualification step before accepting a candidate.

Actual e-commerce stores selling physical or digital products.
DTC brands.
Online retailers.
E-commerce businesses.

From:

Agencies.
Consultants.
Financial service providers.
SaaS companies.
Marketing companies.
General B2B service providers.

We should address this before considering autonomous discovery reliable.
There is also a small UI inconsistency: the new discovery button says Discover 30 Companies, while the backend currently caps discovery at 12 new candidates per request.
